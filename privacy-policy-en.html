1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when using Sign Territory Manager. The app is free and designed for registered users who manage territory assignments within their congregation.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: name, email, password (stored securely), and congregation number.
  • Operational Data: territories, addresses, assignments, S-13 form entries, and news published by administrators.
  • Technical Data: device identifiers, app version, error logs, and sync data (to keep information up to date between server and device).
  • Images/URLs: if you link an image to a territory via a URL provided by you.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Enable user registration, login, and account management.
  • Store and manage territories, addresses, and assignments.
  • Generate and print the S-13 form for active territories.
  • Send and display news managed by congregation administrators.
  • Improve the app, fix bugs, and maintain synchronization between server and devices.

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on user consent and the necessity to provide the core functionality of the app (e.g., managing territories and generating the S-13 form).

5. Data Retention

We retain data as long as the account exists and as needed to fulfill the purposes described. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your data unless required by law.

6. Data Sharing and Transfers

  • With service providers necessary for app operation (hosting, database, notifications), acting as data processors.
  • When required by law or to protect legal rights.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for hosting or analytics. These providers operate under their own privacy policies. We take reasonable steps to ensure they handle data lawfully.

8. Security

We take security seriously and apply reasonable technical and organizational measures:

  • Secure transmission: data synced with the server is transmitted via secure channels (e.g., HTTPS).
  • Secure storage: passwords are stored using secure hashing methods with salts.
  • Access control: infrastructure access is restricted and based on minimal permissions.
  • Backup and recovery: periodic backups are maintained with recovery procedures in case of incidents.
  • Updates: patches and updates are applied to reduce vulnerabilities.
  • Breach notification: in case of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users and, where required, the authorities.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We recommend using strong passwords and updating them if necessary.

9. User Rights

  • Access your data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Request restriction or portability of your data, where applicable.

10. Children’s Data

This app is not intentionally directed to minors. If you are underage, ask an adult to manage the registration. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent.

11. Cookies and Local Storage

The app may use local storage mechanisms (e.g., device storage) to maintain sessions or preferences. Traditional cookies are not used in the native app; if a web version is released, cookie details will be provided.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. The updated version will be published with its effective date in the app or on this page.
